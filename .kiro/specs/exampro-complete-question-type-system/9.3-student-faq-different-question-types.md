# ExamPro: Student FAQ — Different Question Types

This FAQ answers common questions from students about **MCQ, True/False, Short
Answer, and Essay** questions. Teachers can point students to this page for quick
answers.

Components used while taking an exam:
- Question rendering: `src/components/exam/QuestionRenderer.tsx`
- Timer + auto-save + navigation: `src/pages/TakeExam.tsx`
- Auto-grading (immediate feedback in **Practice Mode**)
- Manual grading for essays: `src/pages/GradeEssays.tsx`

---

## 1. What kinds of questions will I see?

| Type | How to answer | Auto-graded? |
|---|---|---|
| **Multiple Choice (MCQ)** | Click one of 2–6 options. | ✅ Immediately. |
| **True/False** | Click either "True" or "False". | ✅ Immediately. |
| **Short Answer** | Type a short text (one word to a sentence). Usually 1–2 lines. | ✅ Immediately — spelling rules apply (see §4). |
| **Essay** | Write a longer response. Educator will read and grade. | ❌ Graded later by your teacher. |

You can see what type of question you're on by looking at the Badge next to the
question in an **Educator edit view**; during the exam, just follow the input
type that appears (buttons for MCQ/TF, text boxes for SA/Essay).

---

## 2. Taking an Exam — Basics

- **Auto-save:** Your answers are saved every time you click an option (MCQ /
  True/False) or finish typing (Short Answer / Essay). You can close the tab and
  resume later *if* your teacher allows multiple attempts.
- **Timer:** The countdown is in the top-right. When it hits `00:00`, the exam
  submits automatically.
- **Navigation:** Use the Previous / Next buttons, or click any numbered pill at
  the top of the page to jump to that question. You can also use `←` and `→`
  keyboard arrows if enabled.
- **Flagged (if your teacher enables it):** Some exams show `shuffled MCQ. If question
  order or MCQ option order looks different than your classmate's, that's expected.

---

## 3. Answering MCQ & True/False

**MCQ**
- Click exactly **one** option. To change your answer, click a different option.
- In **Practice Mode** you'll see an instant ✓ / ✗ after you click.

**True/False**
- "True" means the statement is 100% correct according to the material.
  "False" means any part is incorrect.

---

## 4. Answering Short Answer — Spelling & Capitalization

Short Answer matching uses rules set by your teacher.

If the teacher has set **Case Sensitive = ON** (rare):
- You must match exactly: `NaCl` ≠ `nacl`.

If the teacher has set **Case Sensitive = OFF** (default):
- `paris`, `Paris`, and `  PARIS  ` are all accepted the same way.

**Short Answer often accepts **multiple spellings or synonyms**. For example, if the
teacher listed both "USA" and "United States", either one scores full marks.

## 5. Writing an Essay — Word Count & Tips

Word counts are set by your teacher. Check the hint under the essay box. You'll see
something like:

> Word count: **124 / 300 – between 100–500 words.

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Below the minimum | The system records "too short" — your teacher will still see the essay and may decide. |
| Above the maximum | The system records "too long" — same thing, teacher still sees text and may score. |
| Inside the range | Essay goes to **Pending Review** and a teacher manually assigns marks. |

Tips:
1. **Plan 1–2 minutes before typing a quick bullet-point plan.
2. Stay on topic; teachers grade on-topic sentences.
3. Rough rule: 1 paragraph per main idea.
4. If your teacher included a **Rubric, use the same keywords.

## 6. Results after submission

Immediately after submitting:
- For **MCQ / True/False / Short Answer scores are computed instantly shown on your **Practice/Results page.
- **Essays stay as **"** "Pending — awaiting teacher grading.
- Once essays are graded, the overall percentage changes.

Look for badges: Correct, Incorrect, Skipped, or Pending.

## 7. Troubleshooting

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Can I change my answer? | Yes, click another option; before Submit click a different option before final submission. |
| "Will my unfinished essay be saved? | Yes — auto-save fires every ~1 second while typing. |
| "My answer was marked wrong but I think it's right." | Ask your teacher to review it (Short Answer synonyms, or Essay rubric). |
| "Can I see which MCQ options shuffled differently for my friend?" | Yes — teachers can enable shuffling (it's normal and correct options, not a bug). |
| "I lost internet while writing essay." | If your browser's offline bundle saves answers locally — they sync once you reconnect (see Offline Support docs). |
