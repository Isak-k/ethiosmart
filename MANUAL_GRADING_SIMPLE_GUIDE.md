# Manual Grading - Simple Step-by-Step

## TL;DR - Just Do This:

### STEP 1: Enable Manual Grading in Questions
1. Go to **Create/Edit Exam**
2. Click on a **Short Answer** question
3. Scroll down to the checkbox: **"Require Manual Grading by Teacher"** ✓
4. **IMPORTANT:** Even if you leave correct answers empty, just check this box
5. Save the question
6. Repeat for all short answer/essay questions you want to manually grade

### STEP 2: Student Takes Exam
- Student answers questions
- Clicks "Submit Exam"
- Status shows "Pending Review"

### STEP 3: Grade the Answers
1. Go to **Admin Dashboard**
2. Click **All Results**
3. Find your exam row
4. Click the **Gold Cap Button** 👨‍🎓
5. Select the student from the dropdown
6. Read each answer
7. Enter a **Score** (example: 7 for 7 out of 10 marks)
8. Type **Feedback** (what the student did well, what to improve)
9. Click **"Submit Grade"**

### STEP 4: Done!
- Student sees their grade
- Student sees your feedback
- Your work is saved

## The Three Question Types

| Type | Manual Grading? | What Happens |
|------|---|---|
| **Multiple Choice** | ❌ No | Always auto-grades instantly |
| **Essay** | ✅ Yes (default) | Always requires teacher grading |
| **Short Answer** | ⚙️ Optional | Check the box to require manual grading |

---

## Troubleshooting

**"I don't see the Gold Button"**
→ The exam has no manually-graded questions. Go back to edit the exam and enable manual grading on at least one question.

**"No questions to grade"**
→ You haven't enabled manual grading. See STEP 1 above.

**"0 pending"**
→ All questions already graded, or no questions require manual grading.

**"I can't find the checkbox"**
→ Make sure you're editing a **Short Answer** question (not MCQ or True/False)
