Scroll Down

See "Offline Storage" Section ← HERE IS THE DOWNLOADS

## Step-by-Step to Find Downloads

### Step 1: Login
- Open the app
- Login with your student account

### Step 2: Go to Dashboard
- Click "Dashboard" in the sidebar
- Or click your profile and select "Dashboard"

### Step 3: Click "Available Exams"
- In the sidebar, click "Available Exams"
- Or click "Exams" if that's what it's labeled

### Step 4: Scroll Down
- You'll see "Offline Exams Section" first
- Keep scrolling down
- You'll see "Offline Storage" section

### Step 5: You Found It!
- The "Offline Storage" section is the Downloads area
- Below that is "Available Downloads" with all exams

## What Each Section Does

### 1. Offline Storage (Top)
```
💾 Offline Storage
├─ Shows total storage used
├─ Shows storage progress bar
└─ "Clear All Downloads" button
```

**Use this to:**
- Check how much space downloads are using
- Delete all downloads at once

### 2. Available Downloads (Bottom)
```
📥 Available Downloads
├─ List of all exams
├─ Download status for each
├─ Download progress bar
└─ Action buttons (Download, Update, Delete)
```

**Use this to:**
- Download individual exams
- Update exams
- Delete specific exams
- See download progress

## Quick Actions

### To Download an Exam
1. Find exam in "Available Downloads"
2. Click **[📥 Download]** button
3. Wait for progress to reach 100%
4. Done! Exam is ready to use offline

### To Delete a Download
1. Find exam in "Available Downloads"
2. Click **[🗑 Delete]** button
3. Confirm deletion
4. Done! Storage is freed up

### To Clear All Downloads
1. Click **[Clear All Downloads]** button in "Offline Storage"
2. Confirm deletion
3. Done! All downloads removed

## Common Questions

### Q: I don't see the Downloads section
**A:** Make sure you're on the "Available Exams" page and scrolled down. It should be visible below the "Offline Exams Section".

### Q: Where do I download exams?
**A:** In the "Available Downloads" section on the "Available Exams" page. Click the [📥 Download] button.

### Q: Can I download all exams at once?
**A:** Yes, but it's better to download only what you need. Each exam takes storage space.

### Q: How do I use downloaded exams?
**A:** Once downloaded, go offline and click on the exam. It will load instantly from your device.

### Q: Where are my downloaded exams stored?
**A:** They're stored in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB). You can't access them directly, but you can use them through the app.

### Q: What if I delete a download?
**A:** You can download it again anytime. Your results are still saved.

## Troubleshooting

### Downloads section not visible
1. Refresh the page
2. Make sure you're logged in as a student
3. Make sure you're on the "Available Exams" page
4. Scroll down to find it

### Download button not working
1. Check internet connection
2. Verify exam has download enabled
3. Try refreshing the page
4. Try a different browser

### Can't see any exams to download
1. Make sure exams are published
2. Make sure you have access to the exams
3. Check your department assignment
4. Contact your admin
