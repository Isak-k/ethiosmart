# Downloads Section - User Guide

## Where to Find Downloads

The **Downloads** section is now integrated into your **Student Exams** page.

### How to Access

1. **Login** to your student account
2. Go to **Dashboard** → **Available Exams**
3. Scroll down to see the **"Offline Storage"** section

## What You'll See

### Offline Storage Section
This section shows:
- **Storage Used** - Total size of downloaded exams
- **Storage Progress Bar** - Visual representation of storage usage
- **Clear All Downloads** button - Remove all downloaded exams at once

### Available Downloads Section
This section shows:
- **List of all exams** available for download
- **Download Status** for each exam:
  - 🔵 **Not Downloaded** - Click "Download" to get it
  - ✅ **Downloaded** - Shows size and download date
  - ⚠️ **Update Available** - New version available, click "Update"
- **Download Progress** - Shows percentage while downloading
- **Action Buttons**:
  - **Download** - Download exam for offline use
  - **Refresh** - Re-download an exam
  - **Delete** - Remove a specific download

## How to Download an Exam

### Step 1: Find the Exam
Scroll through the "Available Downloads" section and find the exam you want.

### Step 2: Click Download
Click the **"Download"** button next to the exam.

### Step 3: Wait for Completion
- A progress bar will show the download progress (0-100%)
- Once complete, you'll see a success message
- The exam status will change to "Downloaded"

### Step 4: Use Offline
Once downloaded, you can:
- Take the exam offline (no internet needed)
- Open it instantly from your device
- Answer questions and submit
- See results immediately

## Storage Management

### Check Storage Usage
- Look at the "Storage Used" display
- Shows total size of all downloaded exams
- Recommended limit: 100 MB

### Delete a Single Download
1. Find the exam in "Available Downloads"
2. Click the **trash icon** button
3. Confirm deletion
4. Exam will be removed from your device

### Clear All Downloads
1. Click **"Clear All Downloads"** button
2. Confirm deletion
3. All downloaded exams will be removed
4. Storage will be freed up

## Taking Downloaded Exams Offline

### Before Going Offline
1. Download the exam (see "How to Download an Exam" above)
2. Make sure download is complete

### Going Offline
1. Turn off WiFi or mobile data
2. Or use DevTools to simulate offline mode

### Taking the Exam
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Available Exams**
2. Click on the downloaded exam
3. Click **"Start Exam"**
4. Answer all questions
5. Click **"Submit Exam"**
6. Results will appear instantly (no internet needed)

### After Submission
- Results are saved locally on your device
- You can download results as CSV
- Results are NOT sent to the server
- You can review your answers

## Troubleshooting

### Download Not Starting
**Problem:** Download button doesn't work
**Solution:**
1. Check internet connection
2. Verify exam has "Download" enabled
3. Try refreshing the page
4. Try downloading again

### Download Stuck
**Problem:** Download progress bar not moving
**Solution:**
1. Wait a few seconds
2. Check internet connection
3. Try canceling and downloading again
4. Check browser console for errors

### Exam Not Loading Offline
**Problem:** Downloaded exam won't open when offline
**Solution:**
1. Verify exam was downloaded successfully
2. Check storage space on device
3. Try going online and downloading again
4. Clear browser cache and re-download

### Storage Full
**Problem:** Can't download more exams
**Solution:**
1. Delete unused downloaded exams
2. Click "Clear All Downloads" to free space
3. Try downloading again

## Tips & Best Practices

### Download Strategy
- Download exams before you need them

### Storage Management
- Regularly delete exams you've completed
- Keep storage usage under 100 MB
- Clear old downloads to make room for new ones

### Offline Usage
- Download exams before traveling
- Download exams before going to areas with no internet
- Download exams for backup access

### Performance

- Smooth offline experience

## FAQ

### Q: Can I download all exams at once?
**A:** Yes, but it's better to download only the exams you need to save storage space.

### Q: How much storage do downloads use?
**A:** Approximately 2KB per question. A 50-question exam uses about 100KB.

### Q: Can I delete a download and re-download later?
**A:** Yes, you can delete and re-download anytime.

### Q: Do downloaded exams expire?
**A:** No, they stay on your device until you delete them.

### Q: Can I take a downloaded exam multiple times?
**A:** Yes, each attempt is tracked separately.

### Q: Are my results saved to the server?
**A:** No, practice exam results are saved locally only. Normal exam results are synced to the server.

### Q: What happens if I go online while taking an exam?
**A:** The exam continues to work offline. No data is synced automatically.

### Q: Can I share downloaded exams with others?
**A:** No, downloads are stored locally on your device only.

### Q: What if I clear my browser data?
**A:** Downloaded exams will be deleted. You'll need to download them again.

### Q: Can I backup my downloaded exams?
**A:** Not directly, but you can re-download them anytime.
