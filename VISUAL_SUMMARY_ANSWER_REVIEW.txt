    1. Student takes practice exam
       ↓
    2. Answers questions (MCQ, T/F, Short Answer, Essay mix)
       ↓
    3. Clicks "Submit Exam"
       ↓
    4. System instantly evaluates all answers
       ↓
    5. Student sees:
       • Pass/Fail status with score
       • Overall statistics (correct, incorrect, skipped)
       • Per-question review with complete feedback
       ↓
    6. For each question:
       • Status badge (✓ Correct, ✗ Incorrect, ⏱ Skipped, ⚠ Pending)
       • Their answer displayed
       • Correct answer shown (except essays)
       • Feedback message
       • Educational explanation
       • Marks earned
       ↓
    7. Can export results as CSV
       ↓
    8. Page refresh → data clears (privacy)
